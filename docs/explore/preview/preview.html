JobAgent finds jobs in your field, shows how well each one fits, tailors your resume and fills in the application. Then it waits for you to check it and press Submit.

See how it works Open the dashboard
  • Runs on your computerNo account to create. No cloud service.
  • Invents nothingYour resume is reordered, never added to.
  • Never submits without youIt stops before Submit, every time.

See it for your fieldThe same steps for any of 26 job families. Only the example changes.

Frame 1. Hero, with “See it for your field”

The morning arrives
Starts: Cards from ten professions drift in from depth, out of focus, none of them yours.
Ends: One card comes forward and sharpens: the chosen field’s posting, with its score and what the score means.
Proves: This is for many kinds of work, and it sorts a noisy morning down to the one job worth reading.
Changing field
Starts: The front card turns away into the crowd (160 ms).
Ends: The new field’s posting comes forward with its own score. Nothing else in the hero moves. A screen reader hears “Showing the example for Regulatory Affairs.”
Proves: Same tool, same steps, your kind of job. The choice then follows the reader through every chapter and is kept in the link.

Frame 2. Score

The score assembles
Starts: The number reads 0. Skills light up in the posting one at a time.
Ends: Each bar that fills adds its points to the number, so the total on screen is always the sum of what has been shown. It lands on its verdict.
Proves: The score is arithmetic, not a judgement. (Today the number counts up on its own and shows 45 beside full bars if you pause.)
Evidence, then gaps
Starts: The three columns under the score are quiet.
Ends: Each matched skill draws a line to the resume sentence that proves it, then the unmet items appear: one gap, and what the posting asked for outside your profile.
Proves: It never claims a skill for you, and it tells you what it does not know to look for.

Frame 3. Tailor

The resume reorders itself
Starts: The tailored sheet is a copy of the master, in the master’s order.
Ends: Line by line, each bullet lifts and travels to its new place; the one that will not fit slides out to the ledger. Then the posting’s terms turn bold where they already were.
Proves: Tailoring is reordering and emphasis. Every sentence on the right exists on the left.
The ledger settles
Starts: Five counters at zero.
Ends: They count to the moves just watched. “Added” stays at 0 and is ringed.
Proves: The claim “nothing invented” is a number you can check, on every resume.

What is built, and what is not yet

64 capabilities, each marked with how finished it is. Optional ones are complete and switched off. Planned ones do not exist yet.

Apply

13 capabilities
  • Approval queue Nothing is filled until you approve, and the approval covers exactly what you saw. Implemented
  • Auto-fill readiness Before filling: does this employer need an account, and which questions are unanswered? Implemented
  • Assisted form filling Fills the live form in a browser you can watch, and stops before Submit. Implemented
  • Work history and educationNew Fills repeated history sections. Tested on a test page only, not yet on a live employer site. Implemented
  • Answer once, reuse everywhere Your answers are kept per person and fill every later form. Implemented
  • Sign in once, retry all Jobs stopped at an employer sign-in are grouped, with one button to retry them. Implemented
  • Dry runNew Shows what would be filled. Changes nothing on the application page. Implemented
  • Drafted answers to open questionsNew A model drafts from your resume. You approve each one, per job. Optional
  • Wait at a sign-inNew Waits while you clear a sign-in or CAPTCHA yourself, then carries on. Optional
  • Several applications at onceNew Up to three, never two at one employer. Command line only. Optional
  • Browser extension Fills an approved application in the tab you already have open. Optional
  • Coverage probe Reads a saved application page and reports what auto-fill could answer. Experimental
  • Final auto-submit Refused on every application system until a supervised live run is recorded. None is. Experimental

New arrived in version 0.2.3 or later.

Track

8 capabilities
  • Applications board Stages, outcomes, notes and follow-up reminders. Implemented
  • Today What needs you now: approvals, follow-ups due, sign-ins. Implemented
  • What gets interviewsNew Outcomes by role family and by resume, once you have recorded some. Implemented
  • Interview prep packNew One printable page, quoted from the posting and your resume. Implemented
  • Exports A spreadsheet of every job, a tracker, and follow-ups as a calendar file. Implemented
  • Email replies Reads your inbox, never changes it, and suggests outcomes. Optional
  • Follow-up alerts A desktop notification when a reminder comes due. Optional
  • Google Calendar sync Follow-ups written straight to a calendar. Planned
